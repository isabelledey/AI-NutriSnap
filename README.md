# 🍎 NutriSnap: AI-Powered Nutrition Assistant

**NutriSnap** transforms the way you track your diet. By leveraging Generative AI and Computer Vision, NutriSnap analyzes food images to provide instant macronutrient breakdowns and generates personalized daily diet plans tailored to your goals.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

- **Visual Food Analysis**: Capture or upload a photo of your meal, and the AI identifies ingredients and estimates nutritional values.
- **AI Diet Planning**: Automatically generates a personalized diet plan based on your daily caloric needs and nutritional targets.
- **Macronutrient Breakdown**: Real-time tracking of proteins, carbohydrates, and fats.
- **Interactive UI**: A sleek, modern dashboard built for a seamless user experience.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **Frontend**: Next.js (Vite), Tailwind **CSS**, Framer Motion.
- **AI/ML**: Integration with advanced Generative AI models (Gemini **API**) for image recognition and logic.
- **Backend & Auth**: Supabase for data persistence and secure user management.
- **Deployment**: Vercel.
